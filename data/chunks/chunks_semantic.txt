## 1. Remote Work Policy

### 1.1 Eligibility and Philosophy

Acme Corp believes that great work can happen anywhere, provided that the conditions for focus,
collaboration, and security are met, and this belief has shaped our hybrid model since 2021 when
the company first moved away from a mandatory five-day office presence; employees whose roles
do not require physical access to labs, warehouses, or customer sites are eligible to work remotely
for up to three days per week after completing their 90-day probation period, although managers
retain the discretion to request in-office attendance for onboarding sessions, quarterly planning
events, client workshops, and any situation where in-person collaboration is judged to be materially
better for the outcome, and employees who wish to work remotely for more than three days per

## 3. Leave and Time Off

### 3.1 Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid annual leave per month, totaling 21 days per
calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried forward into the first quarter of the following
year; any remaining days expire on March 31.

### 3.2 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for
absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

### 3.3 Requesting Leave

1. Submit the request in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for planned leave.
2. Your manager approves or declines within 3 working days.

## 7. Code of Conduct

### 7.1 Respectful Workplace

Acme Corp is committed to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. This
commitment applies in offices, at client sites, at company events, during business travel, and in all
digital channels including Slack, email, and video calls. Behaviour that would be inappropriate in a
meeting room is equally inappropriate in a direct message.
